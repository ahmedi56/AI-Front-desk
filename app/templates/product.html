What it does for you

It handles the customers who contact you when you can't pick up, and hands you everything else.

Books real appointments

Offers free times from your opening hours and books them. Two customers can never get the same slot.

Sends emergencies to you

Gas smell, fire or smoke: it tells the customer to call 911 and alerts you right away.

Saves every lead

When it can't finish a request, it saves the name, number and need so you can call back.

Off with one click

A kill switch on your dashboard. When it's off, every customer is told a person will get back to them.

What it will never do

Your name is on every answer, so these rules are built in. A customer's message cannot change them.

  • ✕Invent a price. It only quotes the prices you write.
  • ✕Pretend to be human. It says it's an AI.
  • ✕Give repair advice for gas, fire or injuries.
  • ✕Confirm a booking the calendar didn't accept.
  • ✕Ask for card details or payments.
  • ✕Show one customer another customer's details.
